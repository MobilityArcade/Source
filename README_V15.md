FLASH DANZE V15 — FOUR WORLDS (WEB)

Four worlds only: Neon Burst, Pulse Rings, Dance Floor, Neon Temple.
No sound-effect setting and no audio output. No white outer border or rounded frame.

Each world includes TWO ORIGINAL, FULL-RESOLUTION RENDERS:
  worlds/<name>.jpg — 3840 x 2160 landscape
  worlds/portrait/<name>.jpg — 2160 x 3840 portrait

These are original high-resolution geometric/vector-style arcade artworks, NOT photographs and NOT upscaled thumbnails. They are generated from scalable shapes and rendered natively at 4K. Visual preference is subjective: please test on an actual iPhone. Some worlds also have restrained real-time light movement, plus a brief cue pulse. These assets are relatively large; a first load may take longer on cellular.

INSTALL: Unzip and deploy all CONTENTS of the flashv13 folder at your website root, including worlds/ and worlds/portrait/. Redeploy and hard-refresh the site on iPhone. The original old JPG worlds are removed.

To reduce bundle size later, convert JPGs to AVIF/WebP with appropriate responsive fallbacks. Current package intentionally favors image detail.
